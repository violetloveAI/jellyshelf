隐私说明简体中文

01 · 适用范围

JellyShelf 是免费、无需 JellyShelf 账号的个人收藏资料工具。本页适用于 JellyShelf 网页和 iPhone 应用，说明本机资料、备份及外部服务的数据处理方式；具体功能以你所使用的版本为准。

演示图册中的商品、品相和金额用于体验，不代表你的库存或真实交易。

02 · 资料和照片保存在哪里

你录入的商品名称、货号、尺寸、品相、金额、备注、成交样本和保存到资料库的照片留在当前设备。网页将资料与照片保存在当前浏览器的 IndexedDB；iPhone 应用将这些资料保存在本机应用容器中。界面偏好和图片特征也保存在本机。

日常资料管理不会将这些库存内容或照片上传到我们的服务器。我们不提供账号、云同步，也无法远程查看或替你找回本机资料。

照片查找在设备上生成特征并与当前图册中的照片比对。用于查询的照片不会自动保存成一条商品；外观相似结果不是身份、真伪、尺寸或品相鉴定。

03 · 哪些操作会联网

网页需要从托管服务加载页面、脚本及识别模型；演示图册和官网参考照片会向 Jellycat 官方图片 CDN 发出通常的图片请求。托管服务或图片服务可能接收 IP 地址、浏览器信息等通常请求信息。因此，本机处理不等于整个网站完全不联网。

iPhone 应用的运行代码和照片匹配模型随安装包提供。若你恢复的记录引用远程官网图片，显示这些图片仍会请求相应图片服务；系统从 iCloud 获取照片或保存文件时，也由你所选的系统服务处理。

你打开 Jellycat 官网、eBay、Whatnot、GitHub 等外链时，会进入相应服务，其隐私政策适用。我们没有接入自动 eBay / Whatnot 历史成交抓取。

04 · 备份、迁移与删除

手动导出先在本机生成 JSON 备份，再由你选择保存或分享位置。iPhone 分享过程中会使用一份本机临时缓存，分享结束后应用会尝试清除这份缓存。备份含商品资料、已保存的成交记录和商品引用的本机照片；官网图片保留链接。备份不经过我们的服务器，也未由应用额外加密，请只存放或分享给你信任的位置。

iOS 系统可能按你的设备备份设置保存应用数据；你也可以自行将导出文件存入“文件”、iCloud 或其他服务。这些由你及所选服务管理，不是 JellyShelf 云同步，我们无法读取你的系统备份。

删除商品会移除商品记录，部分本机照片或识别缓存可能仍保留。清除网站数据会移除网页本机资料与缓存；卸载 iOS 应用可能移除应用本机资料。导出的文件和系统备份需另行管理，不会随着删除商品一并删除。

05 · 权限与分析

拍摄照片或选择相册图片时，系统会根据操作提供相机或照片选择权限。你可以拒绝，并继续手工管理资料；拒绝相关权限会影响对应照片功能。

JellyShelf 没有接入广告或用户行为分析 SDK，也不出售库存资料。下面的 TestFlight 诊断属于 Apple 测试分发流程，与应用自己的分析 SDK 不同。

06 · TestFlight 测试例外

如通过 TestFlight 测试，Apple 会自动收集崩溃日志和使用信息，并向开发者分享；你主动提交的反馈、截图也可能被分享。可见的身份信息取决于邀请与反馈方式。请不要在反馈截图中包含不愿分享的库存、照片或账号信息。

我们仅将收到的测试诊断和反馈用于排查问题与改进应用，不向第三方转交。处理以解决测试问题所需为限；Apple 的留存与控制方式见 TestFlight 与隐私。这不表示日常库存会自动上传。

07 · 联系与更新

支持及隐私问题请通过 JellyShelf GitHub Issues 联系。Issues 通常是公开的；不要附上完整备份、私人照片、订单凭证、密码或测试者信息。你主动发布的内容由 GitHub 处理，也会供我们阅读和回复。

如功能或数据处理方式改变，我们会更新本页。当前说明更新于 2026 年 9 月 27 日。

PrivacyENGLISH

01 · Scope

JellyShelf is a free personal collection tool with no JellyShelf account. This page applies to the JellyShelf website and iPhone app and explains local records, backups and external services. Available features depend on the version you use.

Products, conditions and amounts in the demo catalog are for demonstration. They are not your inventory or records of real transactions.

02 · Your collection and photos

Product names, SKUs, sizes, conditions, amounts, notes, sale samples and photos saved to your catalog stay on the current device. The website stores records and photos in the current browser's IndexedDB; the iPhone app stores them in its local app container. Preferences and derived image features are also stored locally.

Normal catalog use does not upload your inventory or photos to our servers. We provide no account or cloud sync and cannot remotely view or recover your local catalog.

Photo search computes image features on your device and compares them with photos in the current catalog. A query photo is not automatically saved as a product. Similarity results do not verify identity, authenticity, size or condition.

03 · Network requests and external services

The website loads its pages, scripts and recognition model from its hosting service. Demo and reference photos make ordinary image requests to Jellycat's official image CDN. Hosting and image services may receive normal request information such as your IP address and browser details. Local processing does not mean that the entire website never connects to the internet.

The iPhone app bundles its runtime code and photo-matching model. If restored records link to remote official images, displaying them still requests those image services. Photos retrieved from iCloud or files saved through system services are handled by the services you select.

Opening a Jellycat, eBay, Whatnot, GitHub or other external link takes you to that service under its own privacy policy. We do not provide automated scraping of eBay or Whatnot historical sales.

04 · Backups, transfers and deletion

Manual export first creates a JSON backup locally, then lets you choose where to save or share it. iPhone sharing uses a temporary local cache copy, which the app attempts to remove when sharing ends. It includes catalog records, saved sale samples and local photos referenced by saved products; official images remain links. Backups do not pass through our servers and are not additionally encrypted by JellyShelf. Store or share them only in places you trust.

iOS may include app data in a system backup according to your device settings. You may also save an export to Files, iCloud or another service. These copies are controlled by you and the selected service, not by JellyShelf cloud sync. We cannot read your system backups.

Deleting a product removes its record, but some local photos or recognition caches may remain. Clearing website data removes the website's local data and caches; uninstalling the iOS app may remove its local data. Exported files and system backups must be managed separately.

05 · Permissions and analytics

The system handles camera or photo-selection permissions when you choose the corresponding feature. You can decline and continue managing records manually; the related photo feature may then be unavailable.

JellyShelf includes no advertising or user-behavior analytics SDK and does not sell your inventory data. TestFlight diagnostics described below are part of Apple's testing service, not an analytics SDK added to the app.

06 · TestFlight testing

For TestFlight builds, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the developer. Feedback and screenshots you submit may also be shared. The identity information visible to the developer depends on how you were invited and submitted feedback. Avoid including private collection, photo or account information in screenshots.

We use received diagnostics and feedback only to troubleshoot and improve the app, do not forward them to third parties, and limit handling to what is needed to resolve testing issues. Apple's retention and controls are described in TestFlight & Privacy. This does not mean your everyday catalog is automatically uploaded.

07 · Contact and updates

Use JellyShelf GitHub Issues for support or privacy questions. Issues are normally public. Do not attach full backups, private photos, order receipts, passwords or tester information. Content you choose to post is handled by GitHub and can be read and answered by us.

We will update this page if features or data handling change. Last updated: September 27, 2026.
